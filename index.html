Privacy Policy — Wsy's BOT

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Wsy's BOT ("the Bot") is a Discord application that provides music playback, birthday greetings, mention features, social-media embeds, fun commands, and server utilities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot may process, why it is processed, how it is stored, and how users can request its deletion.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, the Bot may process:

  • Discord identifiers: User IDs, server IDs, and channel IDs.
  • Birthday information: Birthdays saved for scheduled greetings.
  • Server configuration: Channel settings, mention settings, and music preferences.
  • Command data: Commands and information submitted through interactions.
  • Music information: Track names, URLs, queue information, and playback settings.
  • Social-media links: URLs submitted for supported embeds.
  • Operational information: Technical logs used to maintain and troubleshoot the Bot.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate Bot features, schedule birthday greetings, manage server settings, respond to commands, process music requests, generate supported embeds, troubleshoot errors, and maintain security.

4. Birthday Information

Birthday information is saved in the Bot's data files so the Bot can send scheduled birthday greetings. Users or authorized server administrators may request correction or deletion through the contact methods below. Deleting this information may prevent future birthday greetings.

5. Storage and Security

Certain configuration and feature data is stored in files on the Bot's hosting environment. Access should be restricted to authorized operators. Reasonable security measures are used, but no internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may interact with third-party services to provide music and social-media features. Relevant information, such as search terms or submitted URLs, may be processed by those services under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide features and maintain operations. Users may request deletion of applicable stored information using the contact methods below. Some technical logs or backups may remain for a limited period where immediate deletion is not technically possible.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or Discord's age requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
