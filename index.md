# Privacy Policy — Wsy's BOT

**Last updated:** October 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Wsy's BOT ("the Bot") is a Discord application that provides music playback, birthday greetings, mention features, social-media embeds, fun commands, and server utilities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot may process, why it is processed, how it is stored, and how users can request its deletion.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, the Bot may process the following information:

- **Discord identifiers:** User IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and related identifiers needed to operate commands and features.
- **Birthday information:** Birthdays submitted through birthday commands so the Bot can schedule and send birthday greetings.
- **Server configuration:** Settings for channels, mention features, music, and other enabled functionality.
- **Command and interaction data:** Commands, options, and information users provide when interacting with the Bot.
- **Music information:** Requested track names, URLs, queue information, and playback settings.
- **Social-media links:** URLs submitted for supported social-media embed features.
- **Operational information:** Error logs and technical information needed to maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the Bot.

The exact information processed depends on which features are used and how the Bot is configured.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information may be processed to:

- Provide and maintain the Bot's features.
- Schedule birthday greetings.
- Manage server and channel settings.
- Respond to commands and interactions.
- Process music requests and queues.
- Generate supported social-media embeds.
- Troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and maintain security.

We do not claim to collect information that the Bot does not actually process.

## 4. Birthday Information

Birthday information is saved in the Bot's data files so the Bot can send scheduled birthday greetings.

Please submit only the information needed for this feature. Users or authorized server administrators may request correction or deletion of birthday information through the contact methods below.

Deleting birthday information may prevent future birthday greetings.

## 5. Storage and Security

The Bot stores certain configuration and feature data in files on its hosting environment. Access to stored information should be restricted to authorized operators.

Reasonable security measures are used to protect the Bot and its data. However, no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Bot operators must keep Discord tokens, API credentials, authentication cookies, and other secrets private.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may interact with third-party services to provide features, such as retrieving supported social-media content or processing music requests.

When these features are used, relevant information—such as requested URLs, search terms, or technical request data—may be processed by those services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Third-party services have their own data practices, which are outside the Bot operator's direct control.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant features, maintain server configuration, or address legitimate operational and security needs.

Users and server administrators can request deletion of applicable stored information. Requests will be reviewed and handled within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

Some technical logs or backups may persist for a limited period where immediate deletion is not technically possible.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or Discord's age requirements. Do not submit information about another person unless you have an appropriate basis to do so.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page.
